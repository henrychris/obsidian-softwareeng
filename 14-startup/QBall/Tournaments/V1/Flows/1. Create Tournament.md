# Flow Order
```text
1. Basic Details
2. Competition Format
3. Players
4. Teams
5. Rosters / Team Assignment
6. Groups (if applicable)
7. Fixtures
8. Review
9. Account (if anonymous)
10. Payment
11. Tournament Dashboard
```

Each setup step validates only itself and requires an explicit **Save & Continue**.

---
# Tournament Lifecycle
```text
Draft → Active → In Progress → Completed
```

Visibility is separate:
```text
Published | Unpublished
```
- **Draft:** configured but unpaid.
- **Active:** payment completed.
- **In Progress:** first match has started; structural lock applies.
- **Completed:** host explicitly completes tournament; tournament is permanently read-only.
---
# Draft Behaviour
## Authenticated users
- Draft is persisted.
- User may leave and return.
- Each completed step is saved only after explicit confirmation.
## Anonymous users
- Draft exists only in local browser state.
- Refreshing/leaving may destroy it.
- They may complete setup through Review.
- Account creation/login is required before payment.
- After authentication, the draft is persisted and the user returns to Review.
---
# Step 1 — Basic Details
## Required
- Tournament name
- Description
- Organiser
- Venue
- Start date
- Timezone
## Optional
- End date
- Tournament logo
- Tournament banner
## Rules
- Description max: **500 characters**.
- Organiser is tied to the user's account.
- Venue is selected from the existing Qball venue catalogue.
- V1 supports one venue, but the model should support multiple venues later.
- Venue remains editable after the first match starts.
- Logo and banner are separate optional assets.

Action: **Save & Continue**

---
# Step 2 — Competition Format
## Team Size
Configured here.

Examples:
- 5-a-side
- 7-a-side
- 8-a-side
- 11-a-side

Team size affects lineup requirements and formation presets.
## Supported Formats
### Round Robin
- Every team plays every other team.
- Host chooses number of fixture rounds.
### Knockout
- Single elimination.
- Arbitrary team counts supported.
- Byes supported automatically.
- Host may reorder teams for seeding.
- Host chooses:
  - straight to penalties
  - extra time → penalties
  - host decides per match
### Group Stage → Knockout
Host configures:
- number of groups
- number of fixture rounds within each group
- **Top N from each group**
- **Best M remaining teams**

Uneven groups are supported and teams are distributed as evenly as possible.
Knockout byes are supported where required.
### Live Structure Summary
Example:
```text
12 teams
3 groups
Top 2 per group
Best 2 remaining
8 teams advance
```

Action: **Save & Continue**

---
# Step 3 — Players
Players are created tournament-wide before teams.
## Add Players
- Add individually
- Paste/import list
- CSV import
## Required Fields
- First name
- Last name
- Known-as / nickname
- Primary position
## Optional Fields
- Middle name
- Additional positions
- Photo
## Rules
- One primary position.
- Multiple additional positions allowed.
- Shirt number is **not** player-level; it belongs to the player's team assignment.
- Players are not assigned to teams in this step.

Action: **Save & Continue**

---
# Step 4 — Teams
## Add Teams
- Add individually
- Paste/import list
## Required Fields
- Team name
- Jersey colour
## Optional
- Team logo
## Rules
- Captain is assigned later during roster setup.
- Team names must be unique within the tournament.
- Minimum team count: **2**.
- Team order does not affect round-robin fixture generation.
- Knockout order may be used for seeding.
- Changes are not persisted until explicitly saved.

Action: **Save & Continue**

---
# Step 5 — Rosters / Team Assignment
Players are assigned permanently to teams here. 

A player may have only one permanent team assignment at a time. Multi-team appearances happen only at match-lineup level.
## Team Card Information
- Team name
- Logo
- Jersey colour
- Assigned player count
- Minimum roster status
- Captain, once selected
- Manage Roster action
## Roster Management
Host can:
- search available tournament players
- add/remove players
- assign shirt numbers
- select captain

Already-assigned players are excluded from the available pool.
Unassigned players are valid and may remain without a permanent team.
## Captain
- Every team must have a captain before publishing.
- Captain must belong to that team's roster.
- Captain can be changed later.
## Minimum Roster
- Every team must meet the minimum roster size before continuing.
- Minimum is derived from configured team size.
## Responsive Direction
**Desktop:** teams/selected roster on one side, available players on the other.
**Mobile:** team cards; open one team at a time and use a searchable player picker. They may select multiple players at once.

Action: **Save & Continue**

---
# Step 6 — Groups
Only shown for tournaments with a group stage.
## Initial State
- Groups are randomly generated on page load.
- They are visual/temporary until saved.
## Host Controls
- Drag teams between groups
- Reshuffle all groups
- Change number of groups
- Change qualification rules

Qualification rule is shown prominently.

Example:

```text
Top 2 from each group
+
Best 2 remaining teams
```

Uneven groups are allowed but should show a warning.

Action: **Save & Continue**

---
# Step 7 — Fixtures
Fixtures are generated automatically for preview but are not persisted until saved.
## Round Robin
Display grouped by fixture round.
## Group Stage
Display by fixture round, with group labels within each round.

Example:
```text
Round 1

Group A
Team 1 vs Team 2
Team 3 vs Team 4

Group B
Team 5 vs Team 6
Team 7 vs Team 8
```
## Knockout
Show the generated bracket, including byes.
## Group → Knockout
Show placeholders for future qualifiers.

Example:
```text
1st Group A vs 2nd Group B
```
## Manual Controls
Host may:
- add fixtures
- remove fixtures

Fixture ordering and scheduling happen later in the Matches workspace.
## Structure Summary
Example:
```text
12 teams
3 groups
18 group matches
7 knockout matches
25 matches total
```

Action: **Save & Continue**

# Step 8 — Review
One long summary containing:
- Basic Details
- Competition Format
- Players
- Teams
- Rosters
- Groups
- Fixtures

Each section has an **Edit** action.
## Non-blocking Warnings
Examples:
- unassigned players
- uneven groups
- missing optional logo
- missing optional banner

Anonymous users may reach Review.
If they try to pay:
- explain that an account is required
- open login/register flow
- preserve the draft
- return them to Review
- persist the draft to their account

---
# Step 9 — Account
Only shown if the user reaches payment anonymously.
After successful login/register:
- persist the in-memory draft
- return to Review
- preserve all entered data

---
# Step 10 — Payment
Tournament fee:
```text
₦2,000
```

The Review page contains a **Pay ₦2,000** button.
## Flow
1. User selects Pay.
2. Payment provider checkout opens.
3. Payment completes.
4. Provider redirects to tournament Dashboard.
5. Tournament status changes:
```text
Draft → Active
```
## Entitlement
Payment activates that tournament permanently.

Deleting the tournament:
- does not refund payment
- does not grant another tournament
- does not restore the entitlement

There is no tournament reset action.

---
# Step 11 — First Dashboard Entry
After payment:
- tournament opens on Dashboard
- tournament remains **Unpublished**
- a prominent setup/status banner shows:
  - activation state
  - publication state
  - outstanding publishing requirements
  - Preview Public Page
  - Publish

A separate success message is unnecessary.

---
# 15. Publishing Requirements
A tournament may only be published when:
- payment is complete
- every team has a captain
- every team meets minimum roster size
- every fixture has a date
## Not Required
These do **not** block publishing:
- unassigned players
- missing logo
- missing banner

Host may preview the public page before publishing.

---
# Structural Lock
Before the first match starts, the tournament remains structurally flexible.
Once the first match starts, status becomes:
```text
In Progress
```

The following become permanently locked:
1. Tournament format
2. Team size
3. Participating teams
4. Number of groups
5. Group membership
6. Qualification rules
7. Fixture rounds / competition structure

Operational information remains editable according to the main tournament product specification.
