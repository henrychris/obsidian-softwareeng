# Entry Condition
A tournament becomes eligible for completion when all required fixtures are resolved.

Resolved fixtures include:
- Completed matches
- Walkovers
- Cancelled fixtures where cancellation means the fixture no longer needs to be played

The tournament dashboard should then show a completion prompt:

> **Tournament ready to complete**  
> All required matches have been resolved. Review the final results and awards before completing the tournament.

Primary CTA:

>**Review & Complete**

---
# Step 1 — Final Competition Review
The host reviews the final competitive state of the tournament.

Show:
- Champion
- Final standings and/or knockout bracket
- Group winners and qualifiers, where applicable
- Match completion summary
- Any unresolved warnings

If anything still requires resolution, tournament completion is blocked.

The host may still make corrections at this stage because the tournament is still `In Progress`.

---
# Step 2 — Awards
Before completing the tournament, the host must resolve all required awards.
## Host-selected Awards
The host selects:
- MVP / Player of the Tournament
- Best Defender
- Best Midfielder
- Best Forward

There is no voting system in V1.

Qball does not need to know how the host arrived at these selections. Hosts may conduct voting externally if they wish.
## Automatically Calculated Awards
The system calculates:
- Top Scorer
- Top Assists
- Best Goalkeeper
- Most Yellow Cards
- Most Red Cards
## Best Goalkeeper
Best Goalkeeper is determined automatically from goalkeeper clean sheets.

The goalkeeper or goalkeepers with the highest number of player clean sheets win the award.

If multiple goalkeepers are tied, they are displayed as joint winners.
## Statistical Ties
Automatic statistical awards may have multiple winners.

The system should not force a tiebreaker for these awards.

---
# Step 3 — Final Review
Before completion, show one final tournament summary.
### Tournament
- Tournament name
- Champion
- Competition format
- Number of teams
- Number of resolved matches
## Awards
Show all final awards:
- MVP / Player of the Tournament
- Best Defender
- Best Midfielder
- Best Forward
- Top Scorer
- Top Assists
- Best Goalkeeper
- Most Yellow Cards
- Most Red Cards
## Competition
Show:
- Final standings
- Final knockout bracket, where applicable
- Group results, where applicable

The host can return to previous screens and make corrections before completing the tournament.

---
# Step 4 — Complete Tournament
Primary CTA:
>**Complete Tournament**

Selecting it opens a strong confirmation modal.

Suggested copy:

> **Complete this tournament?**
>
> Once completed, the tournament becomes permanently read-only. Results, matches, teams, players, rosters, awards and tournament settings can no longer be changed.
>
> This cannot be undone.

The host must explicitly confirm.

---
# Step 5 — Completion
On confirmation:

`In Progress → Completed`

`Completed` is the final lifecycle status.

There is no separate `Locked` status and no correction window.

Once completed:
- Match results become read-only
- Match events become read-only
- Lineups and substitutions become read-only
- Standings become read-only
- Knockout brackets become read-only
- Teams become read-only
- Players become read-only
- Rosters become read-only
- Shirt numbers become read-only
- Awards become read-only
- Tournament settings become read-only
- Competition configuration becomes read-only
- Audit history remains accessible

The tournament cannot be reopened.

---
# Public Completed Tournament Experience
Once the tournament is completed, the public tournament page switches from an active competition experience to a historical/final presentation.

Recommended information hierarchy:
1. Champion
2. Awards
3. Final Standings / Knockout Bracket
4. Tournament Statistics
5. Matches and Results
6. Teams
7. Players
8. Audit History

The tournament remains publicly accessible according to its publishing state.

---
# Lifecycle
The final tournament lifecycle is:

`Draft → Active → In Progress → Completed`

`Completed` means the tournament is permanently final and read-only.
